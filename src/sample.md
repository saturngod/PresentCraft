# Markdown Slides
	Write first, design later

Welcome, everyone. This is the cover. Anything without a tab in front of it is what I say, not what the audience reads.

---
## One Idea per Slide

Three dashes on their own line start a new slide. A level-two heading alone becomes a centred title.

---
### Write in Plain Text
	Headings are always visible. Put a tab in front of a paragraph to show it on the slide.

	- Tab-indented lists show up too
	- **Bold**, *italic*, ==highlight== and `code`
	- [Links](https://example.com) and ~~strikethrough~~

Lines without a tab stay in the speaker notes.

### Pictures
	Put a picture's path or web address on its own line. Add size, x and y lines below it to place it.

Pictures that can't be found are drawn as a soft colour placeholder.
